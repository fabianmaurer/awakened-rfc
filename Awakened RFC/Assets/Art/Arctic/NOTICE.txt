Snowy farms, mines, white ice bears, Arctic hunters, Hunting grounds, Deep
archives and ice city houses are modified Civilization IV game assets.
Original models, animations and textures: Firaxis Games / 2K Games.
For use with Civilization IV: Beyond the Sword and this mod.
The Eskimo polar-star flag and procedural igloo meshes/ice-block texture are
original artwork for Awakened RFC. Hunting grounds combine the original Camp
with enlarged Deer resource models; Deep archives add emissive blue crystals.
Medieval and modern city models retain European geometry with ice textures.

Generated by tools/build_arctic_art.py from a locally installed licensed game,
using the same source reader as tools/build_slave_art.py. No external artwork
was downloaded. Texture variants preserve the original model and animation UVs.
