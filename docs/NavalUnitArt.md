# Medieval ship models

The War Galley uses MightyToad's Civ4 conversion of the Civ5 Byzantine Dromon,
including its own animation set and the supplied team flag credited to
PPQ_Purple. Its visual Greek-fire attack has no additional gameplay effect.
The supplied `Art/Units/Dromon/Credits.txt` and integration notes are preserved.
Awakened RFC normalizes static texture paths and includes the fire projectile
and its effect definition so the animation has its required assets.

Source: [MightyToad's Byzantine Dromon](https://forums.civfanatics.com/resources/byzantine-dromon.28243/).

The Cog uses the Hanseatic ship mesh, its three textures, button and Frigate
animation setup distributed by Dawn of Civilization. The local distribution
does not identify a separate original author for this mesh. Both models and
the Cog button were imported from Dawn of Civilization; its
[license notice](third-party/DawnOfCivilization-LICENSE.md) is retained.

Source: [Dawn of Civilization](https://github.com/dguenms/Dawn-of-Civilization).

The [static model preview](../art/navy/models-preview.png) shows the distinct
meshes with their textures. Animation, effects and in-game scale still require
user-operated in-game verification.
